Questions to explore

  1. In “Order matters,” predict the two formulas before swapping the functions. Does adding three happen before or after squaring?
  2. In “Logarithm domain,” test x = −2, 0, and 2. What reaches the logarithm at each input?
  3. In “Square root of a logarithm,” compare x = 0.5 with x = 1. Which stage rules out the first input?
  4. In “Exponential peak,” explain why opposite inputs have equal outputs and why the output never exceeds one.
