Spring Lab — sinusoidal motion

Hang a mass on a spring, pull it down, and let go. It bounces up and down forever (in an ideal world), and its displacement from rest traces a sine wave in time. The pull-back distance sets the amplitude, the spring stiffness sets how fast it cycles, and where you start in the bounce sets the phase.
