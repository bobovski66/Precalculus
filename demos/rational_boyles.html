Boyle's Law Lab — rational functions

Trap a fixed amount of gas in a syringe and push the plunger to change the volume, holding temperature constant. Squeeze the gas into half the space and the pressure doubles: pressure and volume are inversely related, P = k / V. Plot pressure vs. volume and the points trace a rational curve with two asymptotes — pressure shoots up as volume approaches zero, and flattens toward zero as volume grows large.
