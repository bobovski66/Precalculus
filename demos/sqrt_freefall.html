Free-Fall Lab — square root functions

Drop a ball from a chosen height and time how long it takes to hit the ground. Each drop adds one point: fall time vs. drop height. Doubling the height does not double the time — the points trace a square root curve, t = √(2h/g), the inverse of the quadratic free-fall you saw before.
