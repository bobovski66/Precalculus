Inverse-Square Lab — light intensity

Point a light meter at a bulb and slide it away. The same light now spreads over a larger sphere, so the reading falls off as the square of the distance: I = k / d². Move twice as far and the intensity drops to a quarter, not a half. Plot intensity vs. distance and the points trace an inverse-square curve — much steeper near the source than a plain 1/d, and flattening fast as it recedes.
