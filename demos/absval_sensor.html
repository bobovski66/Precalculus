Sensor Lab — absolute value

A cart rolls past a fixed sensor at constant speed. The sensor doesn't care which side the cart is on — it only reports distance, which is never negative. As the cart approaches the distance shrinks; as it leaves, the distance grows again. Distance vs. time traces a V: two straight lines meeting at a sharp corner the instant the cart passes the sensor.
