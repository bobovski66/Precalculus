# Precalculus I
Course materials, interactive demos, and labs for Precalculus I (college algebra) at Centralia College.
The organizing idea is to treat **functions as data** — point clouds with a constraint — and to introduce each function family through data of a characteristic shape. 

## Lecture Notes

1. [Functions](https://bobovski66.github.io/Precalculus/notes/Precalc_Week1.pdf)  
   Relations as point clouds, the function condition, the vertical line test, fitting a model, and transformations `A f(b(x-h)) + k`.
2. [Lines and Parabolas](https://bobovski66.github.io/Precalculus/notes/Precalc_Week2.pdf)  
   Lines as families of similar triangles; parabolas as focusing curves; two points determine a line, three a parabola; the three quadratic forms.
3. [Polynomials and Their Shape](https://bobovski66.github.io/Precalculus/notes/Precalc_Week3.pdf)  
   End behavior, roots and multiplicity, sign charts, and assembling a global sketch from local data.
4. [Rational Functions](https://bobovski66.github.io/Precalculus/notes/Precalc_Week4.pdf)  
   Zeros and vertical asymptotes, degree comparison for end behavior, long division and slant asymptotes.
5. [Inverses and Radicals](https://bobovski66.github.io/Precalculus/notes/Precalc_Week5.pdf)  
   Domain and range, one-to-one and onto, inverse functions, and radicals as inverses of power functions.
6. [Exponential Functions](https://bobovski66.github.io/Precalculus/notes/Precalc_Week6.pdf)  
   Constant-ratio growth and decay, negative exponents, the number *e* by a compounding argument, change of base, and half-life.
7. [Logarithms](https://bobovski66.github.io/Precalculus/notes/Precalc_Week7.pdf)  
   Logarithms as inverses of exponentials, the laws, change of base, and the role of base-2 logarithms in information theory.
8. [Implicit Curves and Conics](https://bobovski66.github.io/Precalculus/notes/Precalc_Week8.pdf)  
   Curves defined by equations, an elliptic curve example, and the four conic sections.
9. [Systems of Linear Equations](https://bobovski66.github.io/Precalculus/notes/Precalc_Week9.pdf)  
   The geometry of solution sets in the plane and in space, and solving by substitution and back-substitution.
10. [Matrices and Gaussian Elimination](https://bobovski66.github.io/Precalculus/notes/Precalc_Week10.pdf)  
    Augmented matrices, row operations, Gaussian elimination, and worked physics applications.

# Precalculus Interactive Demos

- [Function Fit Lab](https://bobovski66.github.io/Precalculus/demos/function_fit.html)  
  Match a transformed parent function to a point cloud using parameters of the form  
  `A f(b(x-h)) + k`.
- [Polynomial Roots and Multiplicity Lab](https://bobovski66.github.io/Precalculus/demos/polynomial_roots.html)  
  Explore how roots and multiplicities determine the graph of a polynomial.
- [Rational Functions Lab](https://bobovski66.github.io/Precalculus/demos/rational_functions.html)  
  Identify zeros, vertical asymptotes, multiplicities, and end behavior of rational functions.
- [Transformations Lab](https://bobovski66.github.io/Precalculus/demos/transformations.html)  
  Practice graph transformations using shifts, stretches, compressions, and reflections.
