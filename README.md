# Precalculus I

Course materials, interactive demos, and labs for Precalculus I.

The organizing idea is to treat **functions as data** and to introduce each function family through data of a characteristic shape.

## Lecture Notes and Labs

1. [Functions](https://bobovski66.github.io/Precalculus/notes/Precalc_Week1.pdf)  
   [Lab](https://bobovski66.github.io/Precalculus/labs/Precalc_Week1_Lab.html)  
   Relations as point clouds, the function condition, the vertical line test, fitting a model, and transformations `A f(b(x-h)) + k`.

2. [Lines and Parabolas](https://bobovski66.github.io/Precalculus/notes/Precalc_Week2.pdf)  
   [Lab](https://bobovski66.github.io/Precalculus/labs/Precalc_Week2_Lab.html)  
   Lines as families of similar triangles; parabolas as focusing curves; two points determine a line, three a parabola; the three quadratic forms.

3. [Polynomials and Their Shape](https://bobovski66.github.io/Precalculus/notes/Precalc_Week3.pdf)  
   [Lab](https://bobovski66.github.io/Precalculus/labs/Precalc_Week3_Lab.html)  
   End behavior, roots and multiplicity, sign charts, and assembling a global sketch from local data.

4. [Rational Functions](https://bobovski66.github.io/Precalculus/notes/Precalc_Week4.pdf)  
   [Lab](https://bobovski66.github.io/Precalculus/labs/Precalc_Week4_Lab.html)  
   Zeros and vertical asymptotes, degree comparison for end behavior, long division and slant asymptotes.

5. [Inverses and Radicals](https://bobovski66.github.io/Precalculus/notes/Precalc_Week5.pdf)  
   [Lab](https://bobovski66.github.io/Precalculus/labs/Precalc_Week5_Lab.html)  
   Domain and range, one-to-one and onto, inverse functions, and radicals as inverses of power functions.

6. [Exponential Functions](https://bobovski66.github.io/Precalculus/notes/Precalc_Week6.pdf)  
   [Lab](https://bobovski66.github.io/Precalculus/labs/Precalc_Week6_Lab.html)  
   Constant-ratio growth and decay, negative exponents, the number *e* by a compounding argument, change of base, and half-life.

7. [Logarithms](https://bobovski66.github.io/Precalculus/notes/Precalc_Week7.pdf)  
   [Lab](https://bobovski66.github.io/Precalculus/labs/Precalc_Week7_Lab.html)  
   Logarithms as inverses of exponentials, the laws, change of base, and the role of base-2 logarithms in information theory.

8. [Implicit Curves and Conics](https://bobovski66.github.io/Precalculus/notes/Precalc_Week8.pdf)  
   [Lab](https://bobovski66.github.io/Precalculus/labs/Precalc_Week8_Lab.html)  
   Curves defined by equations, an elliptic curve example, and the four conic sections.

9. [Systems of Linear Equations](https://bobovski66.github.io/Precalculus/notes/Precalc_Week9.pdf)  
   [Lab](https://bobovski66.github.io/Precalculus/labs/Precalc_Week9_Lab.html)  
   The geometry of solution sets in the plane and in space, and solving by substitution and back-substitution.

10. [Matrices and Gaussian Elimination](https://bobovski66.github.io/Precalculus/notes/Precalc_Week10.pdf)  
    [Lab](https://bobovski66.github.io/Precalculus/labs/Precalc_Week10_Lab.html)  
    Augmented matrices, row operations, Gaussian elimination, and worked physics applications.

## Precalculus Interactive Demos

- [Function Fit Lab](https://bobovski66.github.io/Precalculus/demos/function_fit.html)  
  Match a transformed parent function to a point cloud using parameters of the form  
  `A f(b(x-h)) + k`.

- [Polynomial Roots and Multiplicity Lab](https://bobovski66.github.io/Precalculus/demos/polynomial_roots.html)  
  Explore how roots and multiplicities determine the graph of a polynomial.

- [Rational Functions Lab](https://bobovski66.github.io/Precalculus/demos/rational_functions.html)  
  Identify zeros, vertical asymptotes, multiplicities, and end behavior of rational functions.

- [Transformations Lab](https://bobovski66.github.io/Precalculus/demos/transformations.html)  
  Practice graph transformations using shifts, stretches, compressions, and reflections.

## Physics Scatterplot Labs

Each lab runs a simple physics simulation that produces a point cloud of the shape of one
function family.

- [Linear — Air Track](https://bobovski66.github.io/Precalculus/demos/linear_motion.html)  
  A cart glides at constant velocity; position vs. time is a line whose slope is the velocity
  and intercept is the starting position.

- [Quadratic — Projectile](https://bobovski66.github.io/Precalculus/demos/quadratic_motion.html)  
  A launched ball's height vs. time traces a parabola; the fitted leading coefficient recovers
  −½g, and the peak is the vertex.

- [Square Root — Free Fall](https://bobovski66.github.io/Precalculus/demos/sqrt_freefall.html)  
  Fall time vs. drop height follows `t = √(2h/g)`; straightening with `t²` recovers a line.

- [Absolute Value — Sensor](https://bobovski66.github.io/Precalculus/demos/absval_sensor.html)  
  A cart passes a fixed sensor; distance vs. time is a V, with the corner at the moment of
  closest approach and the slope equal to the speed.

- [Rational — Boyle's Law](https://bobovski66.github.io/Precalculus/demos/rational_boyles.html)  
  Gas pressure vs. volume at fixed temperature follows `P = k/V`, with both asymptotes;
  straightening against `1/V` recovers a line.

- [Inverse Square — Light Intensity](https://bobovski66.github.io/Precalculus/demos/inverse_square.html)  
  Light intensity vs. distance follows `I = k/d²` — doubling distance quarters intensity;
  straightening against `1/d²` recovers a line.

- [Sinusoidal — Spring](https://bobovski66.github.io/Precalculus/demos/sinusoidal_spring.html)  
  A mass on a spring oscillates; displacement vs. time is a sine wave, with amplitude, period,
  and phase as controls and an optional damped envelope.

- [Exponential — Radioactive Decay](https://bobovski66.github.io/Precalculus/demos/exponential_decay.html)  
  Atoms decay at random, so counts scatter around `N = N₀e^(−λt)`; straightening with `ln(N)`
  recovers a line of slope −λ, and the half-life is marked.
