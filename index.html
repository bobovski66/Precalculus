How grading works

Weekly modules make up 60% of the course grade, the midterm 15%, and the final 25%. Within a week, Readings, Demos, Weekly Lab, Applications, and Weekly Quiz are weighted equally. A quiz earns its category credit only after every question is correct.

Your current grade uses only work you have started. Future untouched weeks do not lower it.

Course Help

Saving lab work

Each improved weekly lab autosaves in the current browser. Use “Save filled HTML” inside the lab to download a portable copy containing your answers. Use “Print / Save PDF” when you want a frozen copy.

Quiz feedback

A missed quiz answer stays open. Change it and try again. A correct answer is saved and locked. There is no penalty for retries.

Progress backup

Export your LMS progress if you may switch browsers or computers. Import that file later to restore checkboxes and quiz mastery.
