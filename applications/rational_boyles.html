Boyle's Law — Reciprocal Model

At fixed temperature, pressure and volume trade off so that their product remains constant.

Application: a reciprocal relationship

Goal: Recognize k/V as a rational model and interpret its asymptotic behavior physically.
1. Manipulate

Change the gas constant and current volume.

2. Observe

Watch pressure fall nonlinearly as volume grows and rise sharply near small positive volumes.

3. Interpret

Connect the reciprocal graph with the invariant product PV=k.
